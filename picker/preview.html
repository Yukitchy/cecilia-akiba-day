Course A · A little of everything

First time in Akihabara

If you are not sure what you like yet, this is the safe and fun one. A shrine at sunset, the famous figure building, hundreds of capsule toys, retro games, an arcade, and the main street lit up at night.

The day

  1. 16:00
    Meet at Akihabara StationElectric Town exit. A two-minute map of the district.
  2. 16:10
    Kanda Myojin shrineAnime-themed ema plaques and the shrine from Love Live!, in the last daylight.
  3. 16:35
    mAAch ecute Kanda ManseibashiOld red-brick railway arches at sunset. The best photo spot of the day.
  4. 17:00
    Radio KaikanFloor after floor of figures and goods. See what is popular this month.
  5. 17:40
    Akihabara Gachapon KaikanHundreds of capsule toy machines. Pick a souvenir by luck.
  6. 18:05
    Super PotatoRetro games from the 80s and 90s, three floors of them.
  7. 18:30
    GiGO arcadeTry the crane games.
  8. 18:50
    Chuo-dori at nightOne last photo under the neon, then goodbye.

The route

All on foot, about 2.5 km. Short and flat, with breaks inside the shops. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Good for First-timers, mixed groups, anyone who wants to see the whole district in one evening.

Keep in mind The sun sets around 16:40 in early November, so we do the outdoor photos first and the neon at the end. Closing times will be re-checked once the date is fixed.

Course B · Built around the series you love

Find your favorite

Tell me the anime or games you love, and we go straight to the shops that have them. New goods first, then the used and rare shops where fans find things they cannot get at home.

The day

  1. 16:00
    Meet at Akihabara StationElectric Town exit.
  2. 16:05
    Animate AkihabaraNew official goods for your series.
  3. 16:40
    Mandarake ComplexEight floors of used manga, figures and rare items. Real finds, honest prices.
  4. 17:30
    Lashinbang or SurugayaA second shop to dig through. I choose which one by your series.
  5. 18:10
    Akihabara Gachapon KaikanLook for capsule toys of your favorite characters.
  6. 18:40
    Chuo-dori at nightA photo with what you found, under the neon.

The route

All on foot, about 2 km. The shops are close together. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Good for Fans with a favorite series or character. Collectors.

Keep in mind Send me the names of the series you love before the day, and I will change the shops and floors to match. The sun sets around 16:40 in early November, so we do the outdoor photos first and the neon at the end. Closing times will be re-checked once the date is fixed.

Course C · The old electric town and the arcades

Retro and games

Akihabara before anime: tiny electronics stalls under the train tracks. Then retro consoles, rhythm games and fighting games, and the arcades at night.

The day

  1. 16:00
    Meet at Akihabara StationElectric Town exit.
  2. 16:05
    Akihabara Radio CenterNarrow alleys of electronic parts under the tracks, the way Akihabara started.
  3. 16:30
    mAAch ecute Kanda ManseibashiRed-brick railway arches at sunset.
  4. 17:00
    Super PotatoFamicom and Super Famicom on the shelves, old arcade machines and Japanese snacks upstairs.
  5. 17:45
    Taito Station and GiGORhythm games, fighting games, crane games.
  6. 18:30
    Akihabara Gachapon KaikanA last capsule toy, then a neon photo on Chuo-dori.

The route

All on foot, about 2 km. Open the route in Google Maps ↗

Where we eat

Good for Gamers, retro fans, people who have seen Akihabara before.

Keep in mind The sun sets around 16:40 in early November, so we do the outdoor photos first and the neon at the end. Closing times will be re-checked once the date is fixed.
